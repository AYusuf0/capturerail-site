Privacy notice

Effective 11 September 2026.

What we collect

  • At checkout: your name, email address and the solicitation number you enter. Stripe collects your card details; we never see or store card numbers.
  • On delivery: the time the matrix was sent, the email’s Message-ID, and a fingerprint (hash) and size of the file we sent you. We keep this to re-send a matrix on request and to document delivery if a payment is disputed.
  • When you write to us: your message and address, so we can reply.
  • If you use amendment tracking: which solicitation to watch, until it closes.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

What we do with it

  • Build and deliver what you bought, and answer your messages.
  • For paying customers only: occasionally email a notice of a newly posted solicitation that resembles what you bought. Every such email carries a one-line opt-out (reply STOP), honored within 10 business days and permanently.
  • Document delivery if a charge is disputed with a card issuer.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone except the service providers below, and we do not use it to build profiles for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Stripe processes payments and holds your card details under its own privacy policy.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts the system that builds your matrix, in the United States.
  • Google (Gmail) transmits our email.
  • SAM.gov is the public source of the solicitation documents. We do not send it anything about you.

How long we keep it

Order records, delivery evidence and the matrix itself are kept for as long as needed to serve you and to document the transaction — as a rule, three years from the order, then deleted. Opt-out records are kept permanently so the opt-out holds.

Your choices

Email hello@capturerail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted where we are not required to keep it, or to stop all non-delivery email. We answer within 10 business days.
