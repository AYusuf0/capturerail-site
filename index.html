What arrives

Compliance Matrix
One row per binding requirement: section, the solicitation’s own reference number, the requirement in plain words, the verbatim quote, document and page, whether it’s mandatory, and empty Response / Owner / Status columns for your team.
Coverage
Every source document with its page count, how many pages were readable, how many were recovered by OCR, and how many were analyzed. If anything was not read, it says so in red. You always know what the matrix is built on.
Solicitation
The SAM.gov facts: agency, NAICS, set-aside, posted date, and the response deadline taken from SAM’s structured field — never from a date inside the document.

How it works

  1. Find the solicitation on SAM.gov and copy its number (or the notice ID from the URL).
  2. Pay by card. The checkout page asks for the solicitation number.
  3. The matrix is built from the attachments SAM.gov publishes and emailed to you — usually within the hour, same day for very large RFPs.

Combined synopsis/solicitations and RFQs work the same way. Defense agencies are not served; a defense order is refunded automatically.

Why the quotes matter

A compliance matrix is only useful if every line can be trusted under deadline pressure. Each requirement here carries the exact sentence it was taken from, and that sentence is checked by software against the source page before the row is allowed in. A quote that doesn’t match the document is discarded and counted, and the count is printed on the Coverage sheet. Nothing is summarized on your behalf.

The deadline is the one thing the matrix never extracts from the text: it is read from SAM.gov’s structured response-deadline field, because dates inside a solicitation are routinely superseded by amendments. Everything is built from public SAM.gov data only — no login, no scraping, no data you didn’t already have access to.

Questions

Which solicitations can you shred?
Anything on SAM.gov from a civilian agency — GSA, VA, HHS, DHS, DOI, DOE, DOT, USDA and the rest — with attachments SAM.gov publishes. Department of Defense and service-branch solicitations are excluded, and orders for them are refunded automatically.
What if the attachments are scanned images?
Pages without a text layer are run through OCR. If pages still can’t be read, the Coverage sheet says exactly which, in red. If less than 80% of the solicitation could be read or analyzed, we rebuild it first and refund if it can’t be fixed within 24 hours.
How long does it take?
Most matrices arrive within an hour of payment. A 500-page RFP with many attachments can take longer; it will arrive the same day.
Is this proposal writing?
No. It is the shred: the list of what the solicitation requires, with proof of where each requirement came from. You and your writers decide how to respond. The Response, Owner and Status columns are there for that.
Can I get a refund?
Matrices are built to order and delivered immediately, so sales are final — except where we fail: nothing delivered within 24 hours, a corrupted file, the wrong solicitation through our error, or a duplicate charge, all refunded in full. The full policy is in the terms.
How does the pack work?
Buy ten shreds for $1,190 and you get a personal code by email. Enter it in the promotion-code box on the shred page along with the solicitation number; the matrix arrives the same way. The code works ten times, is tied to your email, and lasts a year.

Do you store my solicitation?
