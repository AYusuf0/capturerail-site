1. What you are buying

CaptureRail (“we”, “us”) builds a compliance matrix for a US federal solicitation you identify by its SAM.gov solicitation number or notice ID. The matrix is an Excel workbook listing the binding requirements we extracted from the documents SAM.gov publishes for that solicitation, each with a verbatim source quote, the document and page it came from, and a Coverage sheet stating which pages were read and analyzed. “Amendment tracking” adds daily re-checks of the solicitation on SAM.gov until its response deadline, with a rebuilt matrix emailed to you when SAM.gov posts new attachments or changes the deadline.

Every matrix is built to order after payment and delivered by email to the address you gave at checkout, usually within one hour and in any case within 24 hours.

2. Refund policy

Every matrix is built to order for the solicitation you name and delivered by email, usually within the hour. Because it is produced on demand and delivered immediately, sales are final.

We refund in full if we fail:

If a matrix is defective — its Coverage sheet shows that less than 80% of the solicitation’s pages were read or analyzed — we rebuild it first, and refund in full if we cannot fix it within 24 hours of your request.

Not refundable: deciding not to bid, not winning, the solicitation being canceled or amended after delivery, or expecting more or fewer requirements than the solicitation actually contains.

If you entered the wrong solicitation number, reply to your delivery email within 24 hours with the correct number and we will build the correct matrix for $49.

Refund requests go to hello@capturerail.com and must be made within 7 days of delivery. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; card refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

4. Scope and limits of the service

  • We serve solicitations issued by civilian US federal agencies and published on SAM.gov with attachments SAM.gov makes available. Solicitations issued by the Department of Defense or its components are outside our scope and are refunded automatically.
  • The matrix is built from the documents SAM.gov publishes at the time we build it. Attachments SAM.gov does not publish (for example, documents distributed only by email or through a controlled portal) are not included.
  • Extraction is automated and verified by software. We extract binding statements — “shall”, “must”, “will”, “is required to” — and treat “should” as non-mandatory. Judgement about what a requirement means, and how to respond to it, is yours.
  • The response deadline shown is SAM.gov’s structured deadline at the time of delivery. Confirm it on SAM.gov before submitting; agencies amend deadlines.
